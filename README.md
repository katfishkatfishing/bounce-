# bounce!
game where you go up

> for haven kl! 

you play as a little slime that has the ability to store gpe (gravitational potential energy), meaning you can bounce!. One day wandering the forest, you fall into a deep pit... Thus begins your journey to ***bounce back*** up to the surface.

## Gameplay
Basically a super short parkour platformer. Bounce back up to the surface! 
To *Bounce*: While **in the air**, **Press and Hold** a **Jump** key, and let go right as you hit the ground. *(p.s. **NOT** pressing and holding from the ground!)*

## Controls
|Key|Action|
|:---|:---|
|A / Left arrow| Move left|
|D / Right arrow| Move right|
|W / Up arrow / Space| Jump|
|(In air) Press & Hold W / Space| Charge up energy for higher jump|
